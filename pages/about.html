Club History

Manchester United is a professional football club based in Manchester, England. The club was founded in 1878 as Newton Heath and changed its name to Manchester United in 1902.

Old Trafford Stadium

Old Trafford is the home stadium of Manchester United. It is one of the most famous football stadiums in England.
